What QRoll is

QRoll runs entirely inside your own Google Account: it creates (or attaches to) a Google Sheet that serves as your private workspace, and every feature — attendance, registers, reports, marks — reads and writes only that Sheet. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what data that involves.

  • Use QRoll only for genuine academic record-keeping you are authorized to perform.
